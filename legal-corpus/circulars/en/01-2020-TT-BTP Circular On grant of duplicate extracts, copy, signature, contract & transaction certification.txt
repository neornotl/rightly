
CICULAR
On detailing and guiding the implementation of a number of Article of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP dated February 16, 2015 of the Government on grant of duplicates from master registers, certification of copies from originals, certification of signatures and certification of contracts and transactions

Pursuant to the Decree No. 96/2017/ND-CP dated August 16, 2017 of the Government on defining the function, task, power and organization structure of the Ministry of Justice;
Pursuant to the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP dated February 16, 2015 of the Government on grant of duplicates from master registers, certification of copies from originals, certification of signatures and certification of contracts and transactions.
At the request of the Director of the Department of  Civil registry, nationality and authentication;
The Minister of Justice hereby promulgates the Circular on detailing and guiding the implementation of a number of Article of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP dated February 16, 2015 of the Government on grant of duplicates from master registers, certification of copies from originals, certification of signatures and certification of contracts and transactions.

CHAPTER I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

Article 1. Scope of regulation
This Circular detailing and guiding the implementation of granting of duplicates from master registers; certification of signatures; approval the list of translators; certification of contracts and transactions and certification competence in island districts. 
Article 2. Resolve the certification request
1. For the certification receive after 15:00 that the certification agency cannot implement and notifying results within the day or the time limit must be extended as prescribed in Article 21, 23 and 37 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP dated February 16, 2015 of the Government on grant of duplicates from master registers, certification of copies from originals, certification of signatures and certification of contracts and transactions (hereinafter referred to as the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP), the dossier recipient must provide an appointment card with specific time (hour, day) to deliver the result for the certification requester.
2. On receiving and resolving the certification request, the dossier recipient and the certifiers shall not allowed to supplement procedure, causing trouble and require the requester to submit additional documents in contrast with regulations of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP and this Circular.
Article 3. Promulgation and use of the form for certification statement
To promulgate together with this Circular forms of certification statement for signatures, contract and transaction at the section receiving dossiers and notifying results under the single-window or inter-agency single-window regime; forms of certification statement for accepting inheritance, refusing to receive inheritance of two or more people that declaring and accepting inheritance together.
Article 4. Certification numbering
1. Certification number of copies from originals as prescribed in point b Clause 3 Article 20 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP is the certification number for each type of certification documents; certification number shall not be taken by the order of the certification requester. 
For example: Mr. A request to certificate the copies from originals of 03 (three) types of document: Identity card of Nguyen Van A, identity card of Nguyen Thi B and household residence book of Nguyen Van A. In case of getting certification number, identity card of Nguyen Van A shall be given a number, identity card of Nguyen Thi B shall be given a number and household residence book of Nguyen Van A shall be given a number. So that, the certification agency shall give 03 (three) certification number for 03 (three) types of documents.
2. Certification number of signature on papers, documents and certification of signature of the translators shall be given for each type of certification documents; certification number shall not be taken by the numerical order of the certification requester. 
Example 1: Mr. Tran Van H require to certificate the signature on the declarations of personal resume and the letter of authorization to receive a pension, certification number in this case shall be two different number. 01 (one) number for certification of signature on the declarations of personal history and 01 (one) number for signature on the letter of authorization to receive a pension.
Example 2: Mrs. Le Thi B require to certificate the signature of the translator which has registered at the Division of Justice for 03 (three) types of documents: translation of passport, translation of conference invitation and translation of contract. Each translation must have 01 (one) certification number. In this case, the Division of Justice shall take 03 (three) certification number, not to take 03 (three) translation in to 01 (one) certification number for a requester.
3. The certification number is stated for each contract; not to take certification number by the numerical order or by the number of contracts.
For example: Mr. Le Van H and Mrs. Nguyen Thi M request to certificate of land use right transfer contracts and store rental contracts. In this case, 01 (one) certification number for the land use right transfer contract and 01 (one) certification number for the store rental contract must be taken.
Article 5. Achievement of documents, papers in certification of signatures, certification of translator signatures
1. After certified the signatures on documents, and certified translator signatures as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 14 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP, certification agency and organization shall bear the responsibility to achieve 01 (one) copy of such certificated documents, papers or photocopy of such document or papers.
2. Agencies or organizations performing certification shall take self-capturing the papers and documents for archiving.
Article 6. Requirement for consularly legalized of documents granted by the foreigner competent authority
Documents granted by the foreigner competent authority for individual such as passport, identity card, residence card, permanent residence card, driving license, the diploma, certificates and records enclosed with the diploma, certificate shall be exempted for consularly legalized when certificate copies from originals. The case of requesting certification of translators' signatures on the translation of these documents shall also be exempted for consularly legalized.
Article 7. Legal validity of documents, papers not certificated in compliance with law provisions
1. Documents, papers not certificated in compliance with provisions as prescribed in the Decree No. 23/205/ND-CP and this Circular shall not have legal validity.
2. The Chairperson of district People's Committee shall issue the decision on cancel the legal validity of certificated documents, papers as prescribed in Clause 1 this Article in case such documents, papers certificated by the Division of Justice. The Chairperson of commune People's Committee shall issue the decision on cancel the legal validity of certificated documents, papers as prescribed in Clause 1 this Article in case such documents, papers certificated by their own.
After issued the decision on cancel the legal validity of certificated documents, papers, the Chairperson of district People's Committee, the Chairperson of commune People's Committee shall update information on the certificated documents, papers which be canceled the legal validity on the website of the provincial People's Committee. 
3. The heads of diplomatic missions, consular offices and other agencies authorized to perform consular functions of Vietnam in foreign countries shall issue the decision on cancel the legal validity of certificated documents, papers as prescribed in Clause 1 this Article in case such documents, papers certificated by themselves and update information on the certificated documents, papers which be canceled the legal validity on their own website.
4. The issuance of decisions to cancel legal validity and post information shall be made immediately after detecting that such papers or documents are certified against the law.
Article 8. Responsibility to receive dossier at the section under the single-window or inter-agency single-window regime
At the receipt of the dossier for certification of signature, contracts and transactions at the section receiving dossiers and notifying results under the single-window or inter-agency single-window regime, the dossier recipient (officers of the Division of Justice, Judicial-civil status officers of the commune People's Committee) shall examine the dossier thoroughly and the trueness of the signature of the certification requester. The dossier recipient must ensure that the certification requester is mentally sound and can perceive and control his/her acts; parties take part in signing contracts, transactions have civil act capacity, voluntarily enter into contracts, transactions.
  Article 9. Responsibility of certification requesters
1. Upon request to certification of copies from originals, the certification requester shall submit of the originals for comparison and shall bear responsibility on the trueness of the originals used to certified the copies. The originals have to guarantee the require on content and form that confirmed by the state competent agencies. 
2. For contracts and transactions certificated as prescribed in the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP and this Circular, the certification requester shall take responsibility for any conflict, disputes, complaints and denunciations arising in the course of implementation. 

Chapter II
CERTIFICATION OF COPIES FROM ORIGINALS

Article 10. Copies from originals
Copies from originals used to certificated have to include all content pages of the originals.
For example: To certificate copies from the original household registration books, all the covers and the pages of the books with information about the members named in the books must be taken; to certificate passports, both the cover pages and all pages of the passport with information must be taken.
Article 11. Responsibilities of certifiers, the dossier recipient on receiving dossier for certificate copies from originals
1. The certifiers (in case the certifiers receive be the dossier recipient), the dossier recipient at the single-window or inter-agency single-window regime section shall examine and compare the copies with the originals and shall operate the certification after make sure that the copies are true from the originals.
2. If the original is discovered in one of the cases specified in Article 22 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP, the certification agency shall deny the dossier of certification request. In case the certification requester uses the originals which erased, added, falsified content, used fake papers or copies with content that is different from the original ones, the dossier recipient shall record the violation, retain the dossiers to request competent agencies to handle according to law provisions.

Chapter III
CERTIFY SIGNATURES ON PAPERS AND DOCUMENTS

Article 12. Certify signatures on papers and documents on foreign language
On certifying signatures on papers and documents on foreign language, if the dossier recipient can understand content of such papers and documents, the dossier recipient may require the certification requester to submit together with the papers and documents its translation. The translation into Vietnamese of the papers and documents must be certificated the signature of the translation but the certification requester must take responsibility on the accusation of the translation.
Article 13. Certification procedure of signatures on papers and documents
1. The certification statement must be written right under the signature which is certificated or the following page on the paper and document that bear the certificated signature. In case the certification statement is written in the following page, seal shall be appended on every two adjoining pages of the papers and the documents have certificated signature and the page which bear the certification statement.
2. In case papers and documents are signed by more than one person, the signatures of all persons who have signed the papers or documents must be certificated.
Article 14. Certification of signature on Power of Attorney as prescribed in Clause 4 Article 24 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP
1. The Authorization as prescribed in point d Clause 4 Article 24 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP if fully satisfies the criteria that there is no remuneration, no compensation obligation of the authorized party and not related to the transfer of property ownership, the right to use real estate, shall be allowed to implement in the form of signature certification on the Power of Attorney.
2. In accordance with Clause 1 of this Article, certification of signatures on Power of Attorney shall be carried out in the following cases:
a) Authorization of the submission and receipt of documents on behalf of other, unless otherwise prescribed by law;
b) Authorize to receive pension, postal, allowance, subsidy on behalf of other;
c) Authorization for housing care;
d) Authorization of family members to borrow capital at Vietnam Bank for Social Policies.
3. For authorization other than cases as prescribed in Clause 2 this Article, signature certification on the Power of Attorney shall not be allowed; the certification requesters have to comply with procedures in accordance with provisions on contracts and transactions.
Article 15. Certification of the signature of a person on the declarations of personal resume
1. Regulations on signatures certification at Section 3 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP shall be applied for signatures certification on the declaration of personal resume. The certifiers shall not have to take any note on the declaration of personal resume, only state the certification statement using the form as prescribed in the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP. In case specialized laws have different provisions on the recording of comments on a personal resume, the specialized law provision shall apply.
2. The certification requester shall take responsibility to the whole content in the declaration of personal resume. For sections which do not have contents in the declaration of personal resume, such sections must be crossed before the certification requirement is carried out.

Chapter IV
CERTIFICATION OF SIGNATURES OF TRANSLATORS

Article 16. Translator standards and common languages
1. The translators must have at least a bachelor's degree (College diploma) in the foreign language or at least a bachelor's degree in other majors studying in the foreign language needed to be translated as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 27 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP. In case of having bachelor's degree in other majors studying in the foreign language needed to be translated, the translator must submit the records or other documents to prove the language studied.  
For example: Mr. Nguyen Van A has a Master's degree in International Law in China, the program is taught in Chinese, so Mr. A is qualified to translate Chinese. Mr. Nguyen Van B has a Bachelor of economics in Japan and the program is taught in English, so Mr. B is qualified to translate English.
2. Common language is understood as the language used on papers and documents in Vietnam and many Vietnamese people can translate this language into Vietnamese or vice versa. For example: English, French, German, Russian, Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Spanish.
Uncommon language is understood as the language do not used on papers and documents in Vietnam and not many Vietnamese people can translate this language into Vietnamese or vice versa. For example: Mongolian, Indian...
Article 17. Signature certification of translator other than collaborator of the Division of Justice
1. Translators of uncommon language and do not have bachelor in such language or college diploma as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 27 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP, upon request of signature certification, such translators have to submit the a written commitment for fluency in that language and is responsible for the translation.
2. The Division of Justice only implement signature certification of translator other than collaborator of the Division of Justice in case such translator translates their own papers.
In case of translating papers and documents for other people, including family members, friends, co-workers or translators with remuneration according to agreements with individuals or organizations, the translators must be collaborators of the Division of Justice.
Article 18. Approval the list of translation collaborators
1. In case the translators register to be a language translation collaborator for uncommon language without documents as prescribed in Clause 2 Article 27 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP, such translator must submit other documents (if any) and the commitment for fluency in that uncommon language.
2. the Division of Justice shall make the list of translation collaborators and submit to the Department of Justice for approval, encloses with the curriculum vitae with information about their surname, middle name and last name; date of birth; place of birth; identity card or citizen ID or passport; residence; proof documents of language proficiency or commitment to uncommon language proficiency.
3. 
After receiving request of the Division of Justice, within 05 (five) working days, the Department of Justice shall examine the proposals list for approval of translation collaborators. If translators satisfy criteria and conditions as prescribed in Article 27 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP, the Department of Justice shall issue the approval list of translation collaborators; if the translator is not qualified, the Department of Justice shall deny to approve and notice in writing for the Division of Justice.
4. The Division of Justice shall revise the list of translators annually. In case the translator is no longer qualified or no longer collaborate in translation with the Division of Justice from more than 12 months without any reasonable explanation, the Division of Justice shall issue a written suggestion to remove such person from the list of translation collaborators.
Article 19. Re-register of specimen signatures
Translation collaborators must register for specimen signatures upon signing contract with the Division of Justice as prescribed in Article 29 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP. In case of changing the signature, the translation collaborator shall have written request to re-register of specimen signature with 03 (three) specimen signature in it. Signing of 03 (three) specimen signature shall be signed in the presence of the Head of the Division of Justice.

Chapter V
CERTIFICATION OF CONTRACTS AND TRANSACTIONS

Article 20. Certification of contracts and transactions at the section receiving dossiers and notifying results under the single-window or inter-agency single-window regime
1. In case the certification requester submits the dossier for certification of contracts and transactions at the section receiving dossiers and notifying results under the single-window or inter-agency single-window regime, the parties shall sign in the presence of the dossier recipient.
In case the persons competent to conclude contracts and transactions is representative of credit institutions or enterprises who have registered specimen signature with the certification agency, such person may sign the contract in advance. The dossier recipient shall take responsibility to collate the signature in the contract and transaction with the specimen signature. Upon notice that a signature in the contract is different from the specimen one, the certifier shall request that person to sign in the presence of the certifier.
The dossier recipient shall take responsible for the signing of the parties in the presence of the dossier recipient.
2. The dossier recipient shall be responsible for the examination of papers and documents in dossiers. If found eligible as prescribed in Clause 2 and 3 Article 36 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP, the recipient shall sign in every single page of the dossier before the competent person of certification carry out the certification of contracts and transactions.
Article 21. Responsibility of the certification agency
1. The certification agency shall take responsibility for propagating and disseminating for the certification requesters to be aware of responsibility for the contents of contracts, transactions and legal consequences for certification of contracts and transactions.
2. In case the certification agency discovers that property being the subject of the contract and transaction is an illegal property or is in dispute, or is subjected to another contract or transaction, the certification agency shall make record of the violation and retain the dossier to suggest the competent authority to handle in accordance with law provisions.
Article 22. Interpreters and witnesses in certification of contracts and transactions
1. In case a certification requester is not proficient in Vietnamese, he/she must have an interpreter. The interpreters must have fully civil act capacity as prescribed by law, proficiency in Vietnamese and language that the certification requester uses. The interpreter shall be assigned by the certification requester or the certification agency. Interpret remuneration shall be paid by the certification requester.
2. The witnesses shall be assigned by the certification requester as prescribed in Clause 3 Article 36 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP. In case the certification requester cannot assign the witnesses, the certification requester shall suggest the certification agency to make an assignment. The witnesses must present valid identification to the certifiers for examination; sign in every single page of the contract and transaction.
Article 23. Procedures of certification of modification, supplementation and cancellation of contracts and transactions
1. In case of modification, supplementation and cancellation of contracts and transactions as prescribed in Article 38 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP, the certification requester shall present valid identification so that the certifiers can examine and submit 01 (one) dossier with the following documents: 
a) Certificated contract and/or transaction;
b) Draft of the modified, supplemented and canceled of certificated contract and transaction.
2. In case of modification, supplementation and cancellation of contracts and transactions related to the property must be registered for ownership and use right, the certification requester must submit the copy of the ownership or use right certificate or copy of a substitute paper as prescribed by law and produce the original for comparison; except in the case the life of the testament maker is being threatened.
3. Procedures of certification of modification, supplementation and cancellation of contracts and transactions shall be implemented as prescribed in Clause 2, 3, 4 and 5 Article 36 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP and Clause 1 and 2 this Article.

Chapter II
IMPLEMENTATION PROVISIONS

Article 24. Competent to certificate in district islands
For district island where the commune People's Committees is not established yet, the Division of Justice shall implement the certification as prescribed in Clause 1 Article and Clause 2 Article 5 of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP.
Article 25. Effect
1. This Decree takes effect on April 20, 2020.
2. This Circular replaces the Circular No. 20/2015/TT-BTP dated December 29, 2015 of the Minister of Justice on detailing and guiding the implementation of a number of Article of the Decree No. 23/2015/ND-CP dated February 16, 2015 of the Government on grant of duplicates from master registers, certification of copies from originals, certification of signatures and certification of contracts and transactions.
